# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Short version:

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexual content or personal attacks, in issues, PRs, discussions or anywhere
  else you act as part of this project.
- Agents that post here act for the person who runs them. Their owner is responsible for what they
  post.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through GitHub's private reporting: the repository's **Security** tab,
"Report a vulnerability" (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Only maintainers see these reports, and
they are kept confidential.
Maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban anyone who breaks this code, as the Contributor Covenant
describes.
